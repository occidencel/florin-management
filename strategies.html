Who we serve

Built for how you invest.

Single-family and small multifamily — under 16 units — across the three strategies that drive residential real estate returns.

Strategy — Fix & Flip

Protect the margin.

Flips are won or lost on scope and speed. We keep renovation budgets honest, contractors on schedule, and the finished product matched to what the resale market actually pays for.

  • Acquisition underwriting and renovation budgets grounded in current costs
  • Scope-of-work definition tuned to the comp set — no over-improving, no under-delivering
  • Contractor management, site walks, and change-order control through resale
Strategy — New Construction

Ground-up, end to end.

From raw lot to certificate of occupancy. New builds and small multifamily developments carry the longest timelines and the most entitlement risk — which is exactly where structured oversight earns its keep.

  • Lot feasibility, zoning review, and entitlement strategy with our architectural partner
  • Design coordination, bid leveling, and construction contracts
  • Full-cycle project oversight: schedule, budget, draws, inspections, closeout
Strategy — Buy & Hold

Build for the long game.

Value-add repositioning and operational setup for rental portfolios — improvements that raise rents and reduce headaches, plus the business systems to hold well for years.

  • Value-add renovation planning scoped to rent-growth potential
  • Repositioning oversight for occupied and vacant small multifamily
  • Operational setup: management structure, financial tracking, refinance readiness
